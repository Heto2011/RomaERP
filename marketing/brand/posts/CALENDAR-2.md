# Roma ERP — second 14 days (Facebook / Instagram / TikTok / Reels)

Post daily at 9 PM Cairo time. First comment on every post: `https://romagroup.app/start-trial`
Trial: 21 days, no credit card. Videos are in `marketing/brand/videos/` (MP4, captions burned in, no sound: add your own voice or a soft track; see the note at the bottom).
Screens are real Roma ERP screens; every name and number in them is demo data.

## Rules that stay fixed
- Do not present the e-invoice (ETA / ZATCA), WhatsApp, store or bank-feed links as live: they start when a customer gives us the credentials.
- Do not promise a price, an income, or "the best". Say what the screen does.
- The assistant post waits until the four real tests with the real Claude key have passed (see `docs/first-customer-checklist.md`).
- Anything that costs money is an optional add-on: say so if asked.

## Short English line for each (if you post in English)
Quotation → invoice in one click. First invoice in a minute. Record a part-payment and the balance updates. Purchase order → approval → supplier invoice. Payroll without Excel. Install it from the browser, no app store. 21-day free trial, no card.

## Plan around the posts (weeks 3 and 4)
1. Answer every comment within the day; send people who ask about price to `pricing.html`.
2. Ask the first real customers for a short review (the star icon in the system); approved reviews appear on the site.
3. The agents programme (`docs/marketing/agent-recruitment-messages.md`) continues in parallel: ten messages a day to people who know business owners.
4. Track which post brings trial sign-ups (the console page "marketing views").

## Voice and music (what to ask whoever records)
- A clear Egyptian voice, one short sentence per step, the same sentences as the on-screen captions.
- A very soft instrumental track under the voice, volume well below it. Use a library whose licence allows commercial use (YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay Music) and keep the licence page.
- Keep each video under 20 seconds for Reels/TikTok; the ones here are 10-19 seconds already.
