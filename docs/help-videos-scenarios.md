# Help videos: 8 short scenarios (60–90 seconds each)

Record the screen of a demo company (a phone-size window is best). Speak in Egyptian Arabic, one step at a time. Add the finished videos to the in-app help center.

1. **First sales invoice**: Sales → Invoices → new → customer, line, payment → save → print.
2. **Collect a payment**: open the invoice → record payment → see the balance change.
3. **Quotation to invoice**: Sales → Quotations → new → mark sent → customer accepted → make an invoice.
4. **Purchase order to supplier invoice**: Purchasing → Purchase orders → approve → book the supplier invoice.
5. **Stock count and low stock**: Inventory → items → reorder level → the alert on the assistant page.
6. **Payroll in 3 minutes**: HR → employees → attendance → payroll run → payslip.
7. **Ask the assistant**: "كام اللي العملاء علينا؟", a draft invoice, attach a supplier invoice photo.
8. **Install the app on the phone**: the "Install app" button (Android), Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone).

For each: say what you will do (5 sec), do it slowly, say the result (5 sec). No intro music, no logo animation.

## Scenario for video 5 (stock count), to record by hand on a demo company
Follows the "Physical stock count" help guide, so the screen and the words match.
1. (5 sec) "الجرد عندنا بتدخله إنت بنفسك، والنظام مش بيعدّل الرصيد من نفسه."
2. Inventory → Physical stock count → "+ جرد جديد". Pick the date, the branch, one item. Read the system balance out loud.
3. Type the counted quantity (make it 3 less than the system) → Save. Say: "النظام حسب الفرق وقيمته، ولسه الرصيد ما اتغيّرش."
4. The line stays "منتظر الاعتماد". Say: "المالك أو المحاسب بس هما اللي يعتمدوا."
5. Open "مراجعة واعتماد". Choose who bears the shortage (branch, company, or an employee). Say: "لو اخترت موظف، النقص بيتسجل مديونية عليه."
6. Write the decision note → "اعتماد وعمل التسوية". Say: "اتعمل قيد وتسوية مخزون."
7. Show the "فروقات الجرد" summary at the bottom (biggest loss first). 
Use the demo company, never a real one. Total time under 90 seconds.
