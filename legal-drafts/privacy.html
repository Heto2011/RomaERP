5 AI features

Some features use Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. We send Anthropic only what the feature needs:

  • Business questions: your question plus summary figures from your books (for example totals for the current and previous month, balance sheet figures and cost-centre totals).
  • Expense capture: the expense text you type or paste.
  • Support triage: the text of a support ticket.

Anthropic processes this to produce the answer. How long Anthropic keeps API data is set by Anthropic’s own terms. [Verify against Anthropic’s current commercial terms and privacy policy before publishing; we do not promise “no storage”.] AI answers can be wrong and are not professional advice.

تستخدم بعض الميزات Claude، وهو نموذج ذكاء اصطناعي من Anthropic. ولا نرسل إليها إلا ما تحتاجه الميزة:

  • أسئلة الأعمال: سؤالك مع أرقام ملخصة من دفاترك (مثل إجماليات الشهر الحالي والسابق وأرقام الميزانية وإجماليات مراكز التكلفة).

10 Security

  • Traffic to the Service uses HTTPS.
  • Each customer’s data is kept in a separate database.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Database backups are sent to Dropbox over HTTPS. We do not add our own encryption to the backup file before sending it. [Planned improvement: encrypt backups before upload.]
  • Sign-ins and changes are recorded for accountability.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
