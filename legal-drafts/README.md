# Legal drafts (not published)

`terms.html` and `privacy.html` are bilingual (English default, Arabic toggle) drafts of the Terms of Service and
Privacy Policy. They are **not** copied by the deploy workflow and are marked `noindex`, so they are not live.

Yellow `[...]` items are open questions for the owner or lawyer. Once the lawyer approves and the yellow items are
filled in, move the two files into `marketing/`, add them to the file list in `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`, and
link them from the footers of the marketing pages (the current live `marketing/terms.html` is the older Arabic-only page).
