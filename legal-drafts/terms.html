  • Your data is never deleted automatically. The account can be reactivated with its data intact at any time before then.
  • We delete data only on a request from the person authorised to act for the account (the account owner or administrator). We first verify who is asking and that they are authorised. Once carried out, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Each deletion request receives a confirmation number. We keep a record of the request (company name and code, requester’s email, reason, request and completion times, confirmation number) for as long as we need it as proof that the request was received and carried out.

Data you had deleted therefore disappears from backups on its own within that time; backups are not used to restore deleted data. [Confirm exact Dropbox plan retention.]

7 Location and face verification (attendance)

The attendance feature records GPS coordinates when an employee checks in and checks out, to confirm they are at a work location. It does not track location at other times.

Face verification is optional. It is off unless your company turns it on. When on, a reference photo of the employee is compared with a photo taken at check-in, using Amazon Web Services (Rekognition).

Your company is responsible for informing employees and for having a lawful basis (and consent where the law requires it) before turning these features on.
